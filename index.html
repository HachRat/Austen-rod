What Is Software Engineering?

Software engineering is the discipline of designing, building, testing, and maintaining software in a structured and reliable way. Instead of just writing code, software engineers study a problem, plan a solution, and turn it into applications that people can depend on, from websites and mobile apps to the systems that run banks, hospitals, and businesses. It combines programming with problem-solving, teamwork, and good practices such as version control, testing, and clear documentation, so that software works well today and can keep improving as needs change.
